# CONTENT.md

Content and SEO rules for ArchiPlan Studio. Every public page must follow this file. The goal: **rich, genuine, keyword-aware content with plenty of helpful internal links, so visitors understand our service and search engines understand our site.**

Related docs: `ABOUT.md` (brand copy), `DESIGN.md` (layout), `RULES.md` (code rules), `PRD.md` (pages and features).

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Write for people first, search engines second.** Content must answer real questions from someone planning a home. Keywords support the writing; they never drive it.
2. **More useful content beats thin pages.** Every public page should be substantial: explain, show examples, answer objections. No page should be only a headline and a button.
3. **Genuine, not stuffed.** Use keywords naturally, in the places where a reader would expect them. Never repeat a phrase unnaturally, hide text, or write for robots.
4. **More content, more links.** Each page links to related pages and guides so the reader can keep learning, and each new article links back into the service pages.
5. **Be specific and honest.** Real process, real deliverables, real timelines, real limits (for example revision counts and what drawings can and cannot be used for). Trust converts better than hype.
6. **Help the reader decide.** Explain terms, compare options, show examples, and always end with a clear next step.

### Keyword groups (adjust to your market)

| Group | Example keywords | Used on |
|-------|------------------|---------|
| **Core service** | house plan design, house architecture design online, custom floor plan design, home design service | Home, Services |
| **Deliverables** | floor plan drawing, front elevation design, house elevation drawing, architectural drawings, door and window schedule, section drawings | Services, package pages, guides |
| **Plot-based** | 5 marla house design, 10 marla house plan, 1 kanal house design, 30x60 house plan, 25x50 house plan, narrow plot house design | Portfolio filters, plot-size landing pages |
| **Style** | modern house design, classic house elevation, contemporary home design, minimalist house plan | Portfolio, style pages |
| **Room and layout** | 4 bedroom house plan, double story house plan, house plan with garage, open kitchen layout | Guides, portfolio |
| **Intent / process** | how to get a house plan online, how much does a house plan cost, house plan design process, what is included in architectural drawings | FAQ, guides, How It Works |
| **Local** | house design in [city], architect in [city], online house plan [country] | Home, About, Contact, location pages |

### Rules for keywords
- Every page has **one primary keyword** and **3 to 6 related keywords or phrases**, decided before writing.
- The primary keyword appears naturally in: the page title, the H1, the first paragraph, at least one H2, the meta description, the URL slug, and image alt text.
- Use variations and related terms (synonyms, questions people ask) instead of repeating one phrase.
- Never target the same primary keyword on two pages. If two pages overlap, merge them or differentiate the intent.
- Keep a keyword map (a simple table: page, primary keyword, related keywords, search intent) and update it when pages are added.

## 3. Page Content Requirements

### Minimum depth

| Page type | Minimum content |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Home | Clear value proposition, who it's for, services summary, process summary, portfolio highlights, FAQ, trust signals, 3+ internal link sections |
| Services / Pricing | Each package explained in detail (what is included, what is not, who it suits, delivery time, revisions), comparison table, FAQ |
| Package page (one per package) | 600+ words: deliverables explained, sample images, process, who should choose it, pricing logic, FAQ |
| How It Works | Every step explained in plain language with what the client provides and what they receive |
| Portfolio list | Intro text explaining the work, filter descriptions, links to each project |
| Portfolio project | Plot size, covered area, rooms, style, design thinking (why the layout works), images with descriptive alt text, "Order a similar design" link |
| Plot-size landing page | 800+ words: how to plan that plot size, typical layouts, mistakes to avoid, examples, link to related projects and order form |
| Guide / blog article | 1,000+ words, structured with headings, examples, images, FAQ, links to services |
| About | Story, experience, process, values, credentials, contact |
| Contact | Multiple contact methods, response time, FAQ, service area |

### Required elements on every public page
1. One H1 with the primary keyword, followed by logical H2/H3 sections.
2. An introduction that states what the page covers and who it helps.
3. Body content that teaches something: definitions, examples, comparisons, checklists, tips.
4. A short FAQ (3 to 6 real questions) when the topic invites questions.
5. Internal links (see section 5).
6. A clear call to action to the next step (order, see packages, read the guide).
7. Descriptive image alt text and captions where useful.

## 5. Internal Linking Rules

More content must come with more links, so readers understand the service and move toward ordering.

1. **Every page links to at least 3 to 5 related pages** using descriptive anchor text (for example "see our 10 marla house plans", not "click here").
2. **Every guide links to at least 2 service or package pages** and at least 1 portfolio page or project.
3. **Every service and package page links to** the order form, related guides, relevant portfolio projects, and the FAQ.
4. **Every portfolio project links to** its category, similar projects, the matching package, and "Order a similar design".
5. **Plot-size and style pages link to** matching portfolio projects, the relevant guide, and the order form.
6. Add a **"Related reading"** block at the end of each article and a **"Related designs"** block on each project.
7. Add **breadcrumbs** on all inner pages (Home, Section, Page).
8. Add contextual links inside the body text (first mention of a concept links to its explainer), not only in footers.
9. The footer and navigation expose the main sections: Services, Portfolio, How It Works, Guides, About, Contact.
10. No orphan pages: every page must be reachable through at least 2 internal links.
11. Avoid link overload: links should help the reader, roughly one every few paragraphs, never unnatural clusters.
12. External links (building regulations, standards, reputable references) are allowed when they genuinely help, opened safely and used sparingly.
13. Routes and anchors come from `src/config/constants.ts`, never typed by hand.

## 6. On-Page SEO Checklist (every page)

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Title tag** | 50 to 60 characters, primary keyword near the front, brand name at the end |
| **Meta description** | 140 to 160 characters, includes the primary keyword, written as a helpful summary with a call to action |
| **URL slug** | Short, lowercase, hyphenated, contains the primary keyword (for example `/10-marla-house-plan`) |
| **H1** | Exactly one per page, matches the page intent |
| **Headings** | Logical H2/H3 hierarchy, with related keywords and questions used naturally |
| **Images** | Compressed WebP/AVIF, descriptive file names, meaningful alt text (for example "Ground floor plan of a 10 marla modern house"), width and height set |
| **Canonical URL** | Set on every page to avoid duplicates |
| **Open Graph / social card** | Title, description, and a good preview image per page |
| **Structured data** | Organization and LocalBusiness on the site, Service on service pages, FAQPage where FAQs exist, Article on guides, BreadcrumbList everywhere, Review markup only for real testimonials |
| **Sitemap and robots** | Auto-generated sitemap, robots.txt allowing public pages and blocking dashboard, admin, and API routes |
| **Performance** | Fast loading, optimized images, no layout shift (supports ranking and user trust) |
| **Mobile** | Fully usable on phones; most visitors arrive from social media |
| **Indexing** | `noindex` on client dashboard, admin, login, and order pages; index all marketing and content pages |

## 7. Writing Style

- Plain, friendly, confident language. Explain architecture terms the first time they appear.
- Short paragraphs, descriptive subheadings, bullet lists for steps and checklists, tables for comparisons.
- Lead with the answer, then explain. Readers scan.
- Use real numbers and examples (plot sizes, typical room dimensions, delivery times) instead of vague claims.
- Avoid filler, repeated sentences, and generic claims like "best in the world".
- Never copy text from other sites. Never publish AI-generated content without a human review for accuracy, tone, and originality.
- All facts about regulations, approvals, and costs must be accurate for the target market and reviewed before publishing. Add the disclaimer that drawings are design drawings and local approval requirements may apply.
- Keep brand voice consistent with `ABOUT.md`.

### Keyword usage guide
- Write the page naturally first, then check that the primary keyword and related phrases appear where they fit.
- If a sentence reads awkwardly because of a keyword, rewrite the sentence.
- Use questions as headings when they match what people search ("How much does a house plan cost?").
- Answer each question clearly in the first lines under the heading.

## 8. Calls to Action

Every page ends with a next step that matches the reader's stage:

| Reader stage | CTA |
|--------------|-----|
| Just learning | "Read our guide to ...", "See related designs" |
| Comparing | "Compare packages", "See pricing" |
| Ready | "Start your order" |
| Unsure | "Chat with us on WhatsApp", "Ask a question" |

Link text is descriptive and action-oriented. Primary CTA is always "Start your order" (label from `constants.ts`).

## 9. Content Management Rules

- Marketing copy, FAQs, and page text are stored in content files or the CMS, not scattered inside components. Components render content passed in as props.
- Reusable text (app name, tagline, contact details, CTA labels) comes from `src/config/constants.ts`.
- Portfolio projects, packages, and testimonials are managed from the admin dashboard so content can grow without code changes.
- Blog and guide articles are written in Markdown/MDX with front matter: title, slug, description, primary keyword, related keywords, publish date, updated date, author, cover image, related links.
- Every article shows author, published date, and last-updated date. Review and refresh key pages at least twice a year.
- Keep the keyword map and content calendar in the repo or a shared document, and update it with each new page.

## 10. Publishing Workflow

1. Choose the topic and primary keyword; check it doesn't overlap an existing page.
2. Define search intent (learn, compare, buy) and the next-step CTA.
3. Outline headings and FAQ questions.
4. Write the content; add images with alt text.
5. Add internal links (to and from other pages).
6. Complete the on-page SEO checklist (section 6).
7. Review for accuracy, originality, readability, and tone.
8. Publish, submit the sitemap, and add the page to the keyword map.
9. Review performance monthly (impressions, clicks, rankings, conversions) and improve weak pages.

### Suggested cadence
- Launch with all core pages fully written and at least **8 to 10 guides**.
- Then publish **2 to 4 new guides or landing pages per month**, and keep adding case studies as orders complete.
